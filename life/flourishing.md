## Flourishing

Henri Bergson called it the [élan vital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89lan_vital), a force that drives life forward. Science dropped the force, but I keep the phrase. What drives me is no mystical force. It is the [seeking system](../mind/transcendence.md), the itch to explore and expect, and flourishing is where I point it.

I point it six ways:

- **Physical comfort:** the richness of material life, attuned to vitality.
- **Emotional depth:** the full range of connection and inner harmony.
- **Intellectual clarity:** curiosity and the joy of understanding.
- **Humor and grace:** gentle, sharp, attuned to joy and absurdity.
- **Spiritual depth:** meaning and presence, in a secular sense.
- **Social nurture:** trust and care.

The first three are the [three realms](../mind/three-realms.md), and my ranking of them is still temperament. Flourishing does not mean equal effort. It means none of the six goes dark. Humor and spiritual depth are my additions, and the standard [catalog of character strengths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_Strengths_and_Virtues) files both under transcendence.

The practice is a loop. Refine what the six mean to me. Pursue them with courage and humility, head up. [Accept](personal-growth.md) that I will never arrive, since [things fall apart](../mind/core-challenges.md) and the [hedonic treadmill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hedonic_treadmill) resets every gain. Watch for distraction and drift, which is the [pause](../mind/reflexivity.md) at work, and realign with my values. Then start again, because desires change. What matters is direction, not arrival.
