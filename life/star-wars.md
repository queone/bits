## Star Wars Chronology

The [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars) [films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_films) and live-action [series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Star_Wars_television_series) in story order. The one animated series here is Rebels, because it ties into Andor, Rogue One, and Ahsoka. Story dates count years before (BBY) or after (ABY) the Battle of Yavin, the battle that ends A New Hope. A `~` marks an approximate date.

|------------|-------|--------|----------|

| 32 BBY | Episode I – The Phantom Menace | Film | 1999 |
| 22 BBY | Episode II – Attack of the Clones | Film | 2002 |
| 19 BBY | Episode III – Revenge of the Sith | Film | 2005 |
| 13–10 BBY | Solo: A Star Wars Story | Film | 2018 |
| 9 BBY | Obi-Wan Kenobi | Series | 2022 |
| 5 BBY | Andor, season 1 | Series | 2022 |
| 5–1 BBY | Star Wars Rebels | Animated series | 2014–2018 |
| 4–1 BBY | Andor, season 2 | Series | 2025 |
| 0 BBY | Rogue One: A Star Wars Story | Film | 2016 |
| 0 ABY | Episode IV – A New Hope | Film | 1977 |
| 3 ABY | Episode V – The Empire Strikes Back | Film | 1980 |
| 4 ABY | Episode VI – Return of the Jedi | Film | 1983 |
| 9 ABY | The Mandalorian, seasons 1–3 | Series | 2019–2023 |
| ~9 ABY | The Book of Boba Fett | Series | 2021–2022 |

| 34 ABY | Episode VII – The Force Awakens | Film | 2015 |

| 35 ABY | Episode IX – The Rise of Skywalker | Film | 2019 |

Rogue One ends moments before A New Hope begins. The Last Jedi picks up moments after The Force Awakens ends. The Mandalorian and Grogu picks up after the series' third season.
