## Libertarianism

[Libertarianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Libertarianism) puts individual liberty first and treats force by the state as the thing to minimize. It has real merits: doubt about concentrated power, respect for consent, and a check on paternalism. A popular modern strain extends that into body sovereignty. That is the position that personal choice outranks [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) measures even during an epidemic, and it is the strain I take issue with.

That strain, whatever the intent behind it, is what I call [cold logic](../mind/cold-logic.md): a principle followed past the point where it stops serving people. The body-sovereignty position assumes everyone can make fully informed choices and absorb the consequences alone. That is not the world we live in. Most people do not have the knowledge, money, or medical access of a wealthy broadcaster. Promoting the ideal to an audience of millions during a pandemic shifts the risk onto them.

[Joe Rogan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joe_Rogan)'s public statements discouraging COVID-19 vaccination, documented on his Wikipedia page, are the clearest example of the position in action. The critique here is of the position, not the person.

A political philosophy has to account for [human limitations](../mind/reflexivity.md). Strong institutions exist because individuals cannot reason their way through every risk alone. A doctrine that ignores that cannot hold a society together.
