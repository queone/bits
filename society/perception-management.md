## Perception Management

[Perception management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perception_management), as I see it, shapes public belief less through contact with reality than through stories that are chosen, framed, repeated, and amplified. Accuracy matters less than incentives, timing, emotional charge, and reach. It needs no central conspiracy. Institutions, platforms, and people repeating what feels useful produce it on their own. Some campaigns are planned, like Russia's [troll farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Research_Agency) and [Cambridge Analytica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook%E2%80%93Cambridge_Analytica_data_scandal)'s work for the Trump campaign on Facebook data taken without consent, though Britain's data regulator found its methods [mostly ordinary](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2020/10/08/uk-privacy-watchdog-wraps-up-probe-into-cambridge-analytica-and-it-was-all-a-little-bit-overblown-no/411323). They ride the current more than they create it. Stories are framed to benefit someone, and channels reward the vivid and simple. Repetition breeds familiarity mistaken for truth, the [illusory truth effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illusory_truth_effect). Social pressure makes dissent costly, so weak claims outlive their merit. The [attention economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_economy) rewards outrage, fear, and moral drama because [angry people click and share](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34162706/): each word about the political other side raises the odds of a share by 67 percent. The business side is the [monetization model](monetization-model.md).

Belief can drift far from reality without anyone consciously lying, because the system selects what travels, not what is accurate. My defenses: ask who benefits from the framing. Separate the event from its story. Watch for complex situations squeezed into moral theater. Distrust a claim pre-packaged for instant tribal reaction. Perception management is the struggle to control the story people live inside. Ignore the incentives behind it and you will misread how mass belief forms. How truth can compete is under [viral truth](viral-truth.md).
