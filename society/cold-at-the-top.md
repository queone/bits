## Cold at the Top

Extreme wealth selects for a certain mind: high ability, a focus on systems, the abstract over the person in front of you. [Systemizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empathizing%E2%80%93systemizing_theory) and intuitive empathy sit at opposite ends of one scale, in a theory [tested on half a million people](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30420503/). Power does its own work. [Galinsky](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17201789/) showed that feeling powerful makes people worse at taking another's perspective. Add [incentives](markets-capitalism.md) that pay for self-interest, and indifference at the top is no puzzle.

In a clip [More Perfect Union](https://bsky.app/profile/moreperfectunion.bsky.social/post/3mh6vizvrgk2e) posted, [Marc Andreessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Andreessen) says he has "zero" introspection and that the idea is a modern invention. The history is wrong, since [Augustine's Confessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confessions_%28Augustine%29) are sixteen centuries old. He may have meant rumination. Introspection is the first act of [reflexivity](../mind/reflexivity.md), which is most needed at the top and least seen there. Power removes the correction that teaches it: nobody tells the richest man he was wrong. The clip says nothing about his empathy, and I will not guess.

Both sides fire at this. To the Left: point taken. This is not a moral excuse, only a lens on why extreme cognition plus incentives amplify indifference. To the Right: not an endorsement, only an analysis of the traits and systems that produce the outcome.

Humility is reflexivity's price. The people I see paying it get corrected for a living. The [remedy is rules](oligarchy.md), and [no one person should own the fix](whose-problem.md). [Leaders](difficult-personalities.md) need edge and decency, and the market already pays for the edge.
