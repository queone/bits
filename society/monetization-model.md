## The Internet Monetization Model

The model I see behind most internet content businesses turns attention into revenue through one funnel: traffic, attention, trust, conversion, retention, expansion. It is a modern blend of the [purchase funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchase_funnel), the [conversion funnel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_funnel), [AIDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AIDA_(marketing)), and [content marketing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Content_marketing). It applies to creators, software subscriptions, and online stores alike.

**Traffic** is bought as cheaply as possible from social platforms, search, and communities. It is high volume, low intent, and driven by algorithms. **Attention** keeps people consuming through feeds, short video, and endless scroll. Platforms tune this layer hardest because it comes before trust. **Trust** turns anonymous users into an audience through personality, direct contact, and steady value. Switching costs form here, and they are psychological rather than technical.

**Conversion** is the moment money changes hands: a subscription, a purchase, or a free-to-paid upgrade. It runs at one to five percent, a high-leverage point but not the main profit driver. **Retention** drives [lifetime value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Customer_lifetime_value) through recurring billing and habit, and it decides whether the business is profitable. **Expansion** raises revenue per customer through upsells, cross-sells, and high-ticket offers.

Two versions dominate. Audience-first builds the audience and makes money later. It has high margins and the risk that the money never comes. Product-first builds the product and buys users. It is predictable and expensive.

Ad-funded platforms sell attention directly and skip conversion. Marketplaces depend on liquidity more than on funnels. Search-driven businesses compress the funnel because intent already exists.

The first sale is not where value is created. Profit comes from retention and expansion. The rest of the funnel exists to feed them.

### Recommendation systems

The attention layer is run by [recommender systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recommender_system) tuned to keep you on the platform. Time on the platform is what gets sold to advertisers. The engineers are not villains. They are answering a business incentive, and video feeds, social networks, and 24-hour cable news all run the same model.

The political cost is that the tuning rewards whatever is vivid and stirs feeling. That is how [Pizzagate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizzagate_conspiracy_theory) spread. How that shapes belief is the subject of [perception management](perception-management.md). What it does to political identity is in [identity politics](us-identity-politics.md).
