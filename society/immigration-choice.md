## The Immigration Choice

The case for mass deportation is that unenforced law invites more lawbreaking, and that some towns carry costs they never chose. Drawing a line at the border is one thing. Removing millions of people who already live and work here is another, and I doubt it is feasible, affordable, or wise.

Start with the benefits claim. Federal law bars undocumented immigrants from nearly all [means-tested programs](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/blog/undocumented-immigrants-snap-medicaid-benefits/), including SNAP, Medicaid, TANF, and subsidized Affordable Care Act plans. Exceptions include emergency care and school lunches.

Meanwhile they pay in. The [Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy](https://itep.org/undocumented-immigrants-taxes-2024/), which favors progressive taxes, estimates they paid $96.7 billion in taxes in 2022. That included $25.7 billion to Social Security they cannot collect. Strained city shelters or schools reflect local capacity and funding, not a federal welfare transfer.

Now the cost of removal. The [American Immigration Council](https://www.americanimmigrationcouncil.org/report/mass-deportation/), which favors immigration, puts a one-time mass deportation at $315 billion or more. The same report counts them as about one in seven construction workers and one in eight farm workers. Remove them and labor gets scarcer and prices rise.

If the worry is more crossings, the answer is border enforcement and rules for the future, not expelling a workforce already woven into the economy.

What troubles me most is the cost in empathy. Harsh tactics draw support even from some people with immigrant roots. Yet cruelty toward vulnerable families and children is hard to square with Christian ethics or basic decency.

So the real choice is not open borders against lawlessness. It is whether we formalize and tax the people already here or pay for an enforcement regime with uncertain returns. I would choose a structured, enforceable, conditional path to legal status. It strengthens compliance and widens the tax base.
