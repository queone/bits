## Enlightenment Ideals

[Steven Pinker](../mind/pinker.md) states the [Enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Age_of_Enlightenment) ideal in one line: "we should use reason to improve human flourishing." He said it in a 2023 debate ([part one](https://youtu.be/SA-tCQJEzqk), [part two](https://youtu.be/gNVm-oXFK9k)) with [John Mearsheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Mearsheimer).

I hold the ideal as he states it. This site practices the first half, reasoning in the open, in the hope it serves the second.

He spells out both halves. Reason means [open deliberation](../mind/trust-in-truth.md), science, and history, and its fruits are institutions: liberal democracy, regulated markets, and international bodies. Flourishing is what each of us wants and cannot deny to others: life, health, prosperity, peace, freedom, knowledge, happiness.

He also says what the ideal is not: reverence for some great men of the eighteenth century. "They were just guys." Nor is it praise of the West, which "has always been ambivalent in its commitment" to it.

The rivals are old and still with us. They are religion and its commandments, romantic nationalism and its strong leaders, zero-sum struggle between groups, and reactionary longing for a golden age.

Mearsheimer's objection has weight. Free reasoning, he says, does not bring people to agree on first principles or the good life. It brings "significant disagreement", which is why politics matters. And people are social animals who belong to tribes, today called nations, and identify with them.

I think Mearsheimer is right about how people are. My entries say as much: politics is [identity-first](us-identity-politics.md), and we [read facts through our groups](../mind/cultural-cognition.md). But that is a description. Pinker's line is an aim. The gap between is and ought, [Hume's guillotine](../mind/famous-lessons.md), is where the ideal works. We are tribal, and we should reason anyway.

One caution. Regulated markets are a fruit of reason, but defending them calls for care about historical timing. At times the defense shades into [deference to money](money-exception.md).
