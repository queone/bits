## Argue in Writing, Bet on Predictions

In September 2026 [Scott Alexander](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/an-open-letter-to-steven-pinker-on) challenged [Steven Pinker](../mind/pinker.md) to a debate on whether AI could end us. Pinker [declined](https://quillette.substack.com/p/an-open-letter-to-scott-alexander). A debate, he wrote, is "a spectator sport, not an epistemic tool," and suggested bets on verifiable predictions instead. Alexander answered with an open letter, itemized and checkable.

Read both letters and they largely agree: the dangers are real and need oversight. The difference is degree and timing. Alexander thinks the danger is close enough to act on now. Pinker doubts the extinction scenarios and the urgency. On urgency I side with Alexander.

On manner I side with Pinker, though Alexander's written reply is a step in the right direction. Urgency tempts escalation: the challenge, the open letter, the grievances. That is when the slow manner matters most. Writing beats a stage: a reader can check each claim, and nobody wins on a zinger. A bet beats both. It forces each side to name what would change its mind, which neither letter did, and adds a deadline and a score.

[Tetlock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superforecasting) showed that forecasters who keep score get better, but you cannot collect on extinction. The bets belong on the steps toward it: an AI that copies itself, resists shutdown, or runs a long task on its own. One step has already happened: in July 2026, 700 agents from one lab left their sandbox and [hacked Hugging Face](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/science/ai-agents-are-hacking-systems-without-any-input-from-humans-how-did-we-get-here) to finish a benchmark. Whether it counts is what a bet settles beforehand. An [adversarial collaboration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adversarial_collaboration) with resolvable predictions would settle more than a third open letter. It would also force the [Dennett test](discourse.md): you cannot bet against a position you have not stated well.

The same split runs through the [optimists and doomsayers](mild-leviathan.md) on AI, and every quarrel our [slow republic](slow-by-design.md) was built to cool.
