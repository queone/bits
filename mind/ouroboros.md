## The Ouroboros

I used to picture two worlds, a physical one and a spiritual one, with traffic between them. I now think there is one world, built in levels. [Particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Model) make atoms, atoms make molecules, molecules make cells, cells make bodies, and bodies make minds. Each level is built from the one below and does things the one below cannot. That is [emergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence).

What feels spiritual is the high levels seen from inside. [Three Realms](three-realms.md) makes that claim about the spiritual, and [Transcendence](transcendence.md) makes it about the pull we feel toward it. [Dennett](dennett.md) saw Darwin's idea as the bridge between mechanism and meaning. The bridge runs inside one world.

The ladder bends, though. By size, the [cosmic web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large-scale_structure_of_the_cosmos) of superclusters sits at the top, under the edge of the [observable universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Observable_universe). By organization, the brain rivals the web. [One study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/physics/articles/10.3389/fphy.2020.525731/full) found the two form networks of similar complexity, at scales 27 orders of magnitude apart.

What drives matter up the levels is selection, and not only in living things. [Assembly theory](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37794189/) measures how hard a thing is to build. A [proposed law](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37844243/) says a system gains functional information when selection favors what it does. Both are young and contested. Complexity seems to live at the [edge of chaos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_of_chaos), between frozen order and noise, and the brain [may run there](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/14657176/).

Then the ladder closes into a loop, in two ways. Joel Primack and Nancy Abrams adapt an idea of the physicist Sheldon Glashow. They draw the sizes of nature as a [cosmic uroboros](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/files/PrimackSizeOfUniverse.pdf), a serpent swallowing its tail. Gravity rules the largest scales and, at the tiniest length physics can speak of, rules again, so the head may meet the tail. [Roger Penrose](penrose.md) draws a second loop in [The Road to Reality](https://rationalisingtheuniverse.org/2018/03/27/three-worlds-three-mysteries/): mathematics governs physics, physics gives rise to minds, and minds grasp mathematics. He treats mathematics as a world of its own, a question that stays open here.

Others count other worlds. [Karl Popper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popper%27s_three_worlds) named three: the material, the mental, and the cultural, meaning the theories and art that minds make. He held all three real and irreducible, and [Dennett faulted that](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Popper) as dualism in disguise. On my reading all three are levels of one world, not separate places.

So the duality I started with dissolves. There is one world that folds back on itself, and we are the part of it that noticed.
