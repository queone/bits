## Bruce Schneier

[Bruce Schneier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Schneier) is my go-to expert on encryption and privacy. I value his expert knowledge and his takes alike. With [Pinker](pinker.md), [Chomsky](chomsky.md), and the other thinkers on this site, he helps define my *Weltanschauung*.

I have read [Data and Goliath](https://www.schneier.com/books/data-and-goliath/), [Liars and Outliers](https://www.schneier.com/books/liars-and-outliers/), and [A Hacker's Mind](https://www.schneier.com/books/a-hackers-mind/), and I could not agree more with them. The first shows surveillance as a business model. The second makes trust the basis of society. The third teaches thinking like a hacker, finding the loophole in any system of rules.

One passage in Liars and Outliers stands out. He walks through a day without a gun, body armor, or burglar alarm, and [writes](https://www.schneier.com/books/liars-and-outliers-chapter1/): "It's what we call 'trust.' Actually, it's what we call 'civilization.'" That line cements him as an [Enlightenment](../society/enlightenment-ideals.md) thinker.

On AI he is sober. Its powers are speed, scale, scope, and sophistication, and [Rewiring Democracy](https://www.schneier.com/books/rewiring-democracy/) asks whether they concentrate power or distribute it. An AI "will act trustworthy, but it will not be trustworthy", he [warns](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2023/12/ai-and-trust.html). The task is to make AIs trustworthy services, "agents and not double agents". With Henry Farrell and Nathan Sanders he [argues](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2023/04/ai-to-aid-democracy.html) AI could moderate mass political conversation and summarize what citizens think. That is the hope in [Slow by Design](../society/slow-by-design.md).

On the condition I agree with him. An AI built for shareholders will act trustworthy without being trustworthy, so the fix is AI built as a public good and regulated like one. Even frontier labs ask for rules. Sam Altman [told](https://www.judiciary.senate.gov/imo/media/doc/2023-05-16%20-%20Bio%20&%20Testimony%20-%20Altman.pdf) the Senate in 2023 that "regulation of AI is essential", which concedes part of his point. That condition sits well with mine, an AI [aligned](../tech/before-agi.md) with us that has earned our trust, the [mild Leviathan](../society/mild-leviathan.md). I have found nothing of his to disagree with yet.
