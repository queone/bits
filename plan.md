# Content Plan

## Content Direction

Public static bits site organized as short reference notes, published via GitHub Pages with Jekyll.

## Ideas To Explore

Ideas captured for future reference. A bullet list — each line starts with `- IE<N>: ` (sequential N) for stable references. Two kinds: (a) **idea IE** — `IE<N>: <one-liner>`, awaiting Director discussion and the AC-ceremony evaluation in `AGENTS.md` `### AC-First Workflow`; (b) **AC-pointer** — `IE<N>: <one-liner> → govna/ac<N>-<slug>.md`, pointing at a drafted AC stub not yet through critique. An idea entry that the Director sends to Draft converts to an AC-pointer at AC-draft time, keeping its `IE<N>` number. Remove entries when the idea is rejected, retired, or (for AC-pointers) the AC has shipped and its file deleted. Not a historical record. Governance and director-originated ACs originate separately and do not pass through this section.
